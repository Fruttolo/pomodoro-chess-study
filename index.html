Pomodoro Chess

How it works

The concept

Pomodoro Chess combines the Pomodoro technique with a chess clock. Two timers run in alternation — Study and Break — and only one ticks at a time, just like a chess clock.

Study timer

Set your study duration (default 25 min). Press Start and the study clock counts down. When it hits zero, it reloads and the accumulated break time grows — you earn a break block.

Break timer

Set your break duration (default 5 min). Press Switch to break to pause study and run the break clock. When break time runs out, study resumes automatically.

Chess clock behavior

If the study timer finishes while break time is still available, a new study block is added and the break bank grows. Once both timers are fully used, the session resets and restarts automatically.

Sound notifications

Each card has a bell icon to toggle an end-of-timer chime. Break sound is on by default. Tap Stop sound to silence a ringing alarm.

Picture-in-picture

When you switch to another tab, the timer floats in a mini window via the browser's Picture-in-Picture API, so you can keep an eye on the countdown while working elsewhere.
