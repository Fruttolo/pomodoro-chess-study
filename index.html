Study ⇄ Break

How it works

Chess clock concept

Two timers — Study and Break — run in alternation. Only one ticks at a time. Press Start to begin studying. Press Switch to break to hand control to Break. Press Resume study to switch back.

Study expires → break bank grows

When Study hits zero it does not force you into a break. Instead it resets to full and keeps running — and one break block is added on top of whatever Break time is left. You decide when to take the break; the app just banks what you've earned.

Break expires → study resumes

When Break runs out, Study takes over automatically. Once both timers have been fully used, the whole session resets and Study starts again from the top.

Sound notifications

Each card has a bell icon to toggle its end-of-timer chime independently. Both sounds are on by default. Tap Stop sound to silence a ringing alarm.

Picture-in-picture

While a session is running, switching to another tab automatically floats the active timer in a mini window via the browser's Picture-in-Picture API. It closes when you return to the tab.

Advanced panel

Tap Advanced to expand the stats panel. It shows your cycle count (one cycle = one full Study + Break pair) against a configurable goal (default 4). Adjust the goal with the +/− buttons next to it. The bell icon beside the cycle count toggles a completion sound when you hit the goal.

Streak

The Streak counter tracks consecutive days with at least one completed cycle. Miss a day and it resets to zero.

Session log

Each completed cycle is appended to today's log with a timestamp. The log persists across page reloads (stored in localStorage). Tap Clear all to wipe all saved data and reset counters.
